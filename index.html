Course projects

Sysco Corporation

SYY / NYSE · April 2026

Course project — independent FCFF valuation

Sysco is a mature, high-volume, low-margin foodservice distributor holding roughly 17% of the U.S. market. In 2026 it agreed to acquire Jetro Restaurant Depot for $29 billion — its largest deal ever — opening the cash-and-carry channel it had never been able to reach.

I valued the business on a discounted free cash flow to firm basis, forecasting FY2026–FY2030 from the filings and financing the acquisition the way management said it would: roughly $22 billion of new debt. That shifts the capital structure to a near-equal debt-equity mix, so the discount rate had to be rebuilt on a post-acquisition levered beta rather than the standalone one — a 6.19% WACC against 1.8% terminal growth. The base case is deliberately conservative: growth at the low end of guidance, and synergies capped at the $250 million the company itself guided to.
