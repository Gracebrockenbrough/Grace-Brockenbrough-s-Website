Sysco | Acquisition & valuation analysis

Valuation coursework · 2026

Sysco agreed to buy Jetro Restaurant Depot for $29.1 billion, funded largely with new debt. Had the market priced that correctly?

  • Discounted free cash flow to firm, FY2026–FY2030, built from the filings
  • Rebuilt the discount rate on a post-acquisition levered beta, since ~$22B of new debt moves the capital structure to a near-even split
  • Sensitivity grid across discount rate and terminal growth

$108.72 per share against a $73.24 market price at a 6.19% WACC — a conclusion that holds across nearly the whole sensitivity range.

DCF · WACC · Capital structure · Sensitivity analysis · Excel

Investments | Portfolio management & attribution

Investments coursework · Team project

An eight-week active mandate for a client profile: $1M inherited by a 30-year-old with a long horizon and high risk tolerance.

  • Built a 70–80% equity allocation with bonds for near-term liquidity, benchmarked against the S&P 500, NASDAQ and VASGX
  • Regressed portfolio returns on each benchmark to separate market exposure from active return
  • Recomputed the same holdings under equal-, market-value- and price-weighting to isolate allocation from selection

Returned −20.1% annualized against −17.6% for the S&P 500, at lower volatility (9.3% vs 13.1%, beta 0.68). Re-weighting the same holdings equally returned −4.7% against our −6.5% — locating the shortfall in allocation, not stock selection.

Netflix | Integrated financial model

Financial modeling coursework

A five-year forecast built from the 2021–2023 filings to value the business at year-end 2023.

  • Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow linked so cash falls out as the balancing item
  • Content assets, PP&E, debt and equity each roll forward, so the balance sheet has to tie first
  • Discounted at 10% with 3% terminal growth

$253.3B modeled enterprise value against $219.0B for the market — most of the gap sitting in the terminal value.
