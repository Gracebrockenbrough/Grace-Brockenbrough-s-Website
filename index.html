What draws me to this work

What draws me to this work

Finance interested me first as an analytical problem. What holds my attention in wealth management is that the analysis is never the whole of it. Every family arrives with its own history and priorities — who the money is for, what it should do, and what happens when those answers differ across generations.

I like that the work asks for both halves. You have to be precise about allocation, taxes and liquidity, and you have to understand people well enough to know which trade-offs actually matter to them. I'm curious by default, and I enjoy seeing how the separate pieces of someone's financial life connect to each other.

Sysco Corporation valuation

Valuation coursework · April 2026

Sysco agreed to acquire Jetro Restaurant Depot for $29.1 billion — its largest deal ever, funded largely with new debt. The question was whether the market had priced that correctly.

Question
Is Sysco worth more than it trades for once the Jetro acquisition and the debt behind it are properly reflected?
Approach
Discounted free cash flow to firm, FY2026–FY2030, built from the filings. Because roughly $22B of new debt moves the capital structure to a near-even split, I rebuilt the discount rate on a post-acquisition levered beta rather than the standalone one.
Result
$108.72 per share against a $73.24 market price at a 6.19% WACC. A sensitivity grid across the full plausible range of discount rates and terminal growth showed the conclusion holds almost everywhere.

Netflix five-year forecast

Financial modeling coursework

An integrated three-statement forecast built from the 2021–2023 filings, used to value the business as of year-end 2023.

Question
What is Netflix's enterprise value, and how much of it depends on assumptions rather than observed performance?
Approach
Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow linked so cash falls out as the balancing item. Content assets, PP&E, debt and equity each roll forward, so the balance sheet has to tie before the DCF means anything.
Result
$253.3B modeled enterprise value against $219.0B for the market — about 16% higher, with most of the gap sitting in the terminal value, which is why the sensitivity table matters more than the point estimate.

Most of what I have chosen to do outside of class points in the same direction. I like problems with a lot of moving parts, and I like the people attached to them.
