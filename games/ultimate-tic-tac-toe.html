    How it works
    1. Nine boards, one big board. Win a small board and you claim its square on the big board.
    2. The send. Whichever square you play in, your opponent must play in the board sitting in that position. Take a center square and they are sent to the center board.
    3. Free move. If the send points at a board that is already won or full, your opponent may play in any open board. Handing out free moves is how games are lost.
    4. Winning. Three small boards in a row takes the match. If every board is decided and nobody has a line, whoever won more boards wins; an even split is a draw.
    5. Notation. Boards run A–C left to right and 1–3 top to bottom; squares inside a board use the same grid in lowercase. So B2·a3 is the bottom-left square of the center board.
