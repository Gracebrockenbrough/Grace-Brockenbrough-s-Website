# Grace-Brockenbrough-s-Website

## Projects

- [The Colonnade, parametric elevation](projects/parametric-colonnade.html) - a code-generated measured drawing of Washington and Lee's Colonnade. Every column, opening, cornice and dimension string is computed from a list of driving dimensions in feet; change a parameter and the sheet redraws. Exports PNG and SVG.

## Games

- [Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe](games/ultimate-tic-tac-toe.html) - nine tic-tac-toe boards in a 3x3 grid. Your move inside a small board sends your opponent to the corresponding board. Play a friend on one device or take on the computer at three difficulties. Single file, no dependencies, no build step: open it in a browser.
